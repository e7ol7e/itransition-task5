The video records the real Google Sheets editor, changes to the generator's mine
count, dates, distribution, smoothing and event settings, the deployed dashboard's
refresh, chart options and anomaly register, PDF generation and its native viewer.
There is no narration or subtitle overlay.

The generator is restored to its default six-mine, 180-day scenario after recording.
The sample PDF retains the eight-mine, 150-day scenario demonstrated in the video.
Its source snapshot and detection settings are printed in the document.

The dashboard can generate a fresh PDF for any valid current spreadsheet scenario.
The generator performs its simulation entirely with cell formulas, not Apps Script.
