HOW TO USE
1Edit B4 for mine count, G4:G15 for names, H4:H15 for relative capacity.
2Choose Normal or Uniform in B7. Active labels and moments update in B15:B18.
3Use rho B12 to correlate days; weekday factors E4:E10 and daily trend B13 add structure.
4Configure at least four events in rows 22:27. Change seed B14 to reroll mine selection and noise.
5Open Data for the live formula output and chart. The dashboard Refresh button reads this sheet.
6Output = nonnegative AR(1) baseline x capacity x weekday x compound trend x event multipliers.
MODEL NOTES
Smoothingx(t)=mu+rho*(x(t-1)-mu)+sqrt(1-rho^2)*innovation(t), initialized at mu.
DistributionsNormal / uniform describe innovations; smoothing and calendar factors transform final output.
EventsA Gaussian envelope is truncated to the configured duration; overlapping events multiply.
ReproducibilitySeeded SIN/MOD pseudo-random cells are stable when unrelated cells change; no volatile RAND().
LimitsUp to 12 mines and 730 days. Output is tonnes/day; negative baselines are clipped to zero.
